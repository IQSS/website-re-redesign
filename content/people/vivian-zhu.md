## Vivian Zhu

**Undergraduate Research Scholar**

Vivian Zhu is a first-year interested in applying economic theory to digital governance. She is assisting Professor Mark Esposito with researching the usage of blockchain networks to address misinformation, data privacy violations, and the monopolization of virtual spaces.
