## Soroush Moallef

**PhD Student in Population Health Sciences, Department of Social and Behavioral Sciences, HSPH**

{{< profile-links >}}

Soroush is a PhD student studying social and spatial epidemiology at the School of Public Health at Harvard University. Broadly, his research focuses on promoting health equity and human rights through analysis of how systemic influences, including social, political and historical dynamics, shape population health.  His current projects involve advancing health equity in wastewater-based epidemiology, and historical analyses of the ongoing drug poisoning crisis in the US and Canada.
