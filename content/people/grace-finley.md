## Grace Finley

**PhD Candidate in Public Policy, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Grace is a PhD candidate in Public Policy, interested in research in economic development with influences from labor economics, behavioral economics, and political economy. She is particularly interested in how culture, norms, and behavioral motivations interact with external, economic constraints. In a second strand of work, she is interested in the relationship between subsistence farms' productivity and household-level decisions, particularly investment in education.
