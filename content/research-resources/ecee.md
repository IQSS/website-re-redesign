<h2>What counts as an extraordinary claim</h2>
<p>An extraordinary claim is an empirical claim that sits unusually far from the current dominant view of the scientific community. For example:</p><ul><li>Contrarian findings that challenge widely accepted assumptions</li><li>Novel empirical insights into taboo, under-researched, or dismissed topics</li><li>Investigations that expose structural flaws in prevalent methodologies</li><li>Projects previously deemed "unfundable" or politically risky</li></ul>
<h2>How we evaluate a proposal</h2>
<p>Three things tell us a claim is extraordinary. A project does not need all three, but the strongest proposals carry more than one.</p>

<h2>What an ECEE project looks like</h2>
<p>With ECEE support, Richard McNally's group tested whether trigger warnings and safe-space notices change how students see their instructor. Trigger warnings changed nothing; safe-space notices made students feel safer and more willing to discuss controversial topics, but also led them to see the instructor as politically left. The paper, Sending Signals, was selected as an APA Editor's Choice article in the <a href="https://doi.org/10.1037/xap0000541">Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied (2025)</a>.</p>
{{< quote-card name="Richard McNally" attribution="Professor of Psychology, Harvard University" image="images/richard-mcnally-960x1280_0.jpg" alt="Richard McNally" >}}
“We could have this big design that had the statistical power to detect any differences. [Without ECEE] we could have never pulled it off at this scale.”
{{< /quote-card >}}
<h2>Confidentiality</h2>
<p>The Extraordinary Claims, Extraordinary Evidence partnership is built on trust and an unusual level of confidentiality. All project proposals, data, and research discussions shared with our team are treated as strictly confidential. You can tell others you received our support if you wish, but we will never share anything about your project without your permission. We have found that this high level of confidentiality gives researchers the freedom to explore far riskier ideas than they would otherwise. We invite you to join our highly secure environment for candid, rigorous collaboration with our experts. By keeping your ideas totally private, ECEE can help you develop the extraordinary evidence necessary to convince yourself and the world that you’re right, and if not, to learn why.</p>
