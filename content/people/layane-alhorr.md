## Layane Alhorr

**PhD Candidate, Kennedy School of Government**

{{< profile-links >}}

Layane is a PhD candidate at the Harvard Kennedy School specializing in development economics. She uses natural and field experiments to study digitization's impact on people and businesses in developing countries, with a focus on how new technologies interact with local cultures and norms. During her PhD, her research has focused on the impact of social media on female entrepreneurship in conservative settings.
