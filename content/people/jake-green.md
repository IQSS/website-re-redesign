## Jake Green

**Graduate Student in Public Policy, HKS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Jake Green is from Knoxville, Tennessee and is a proud product of Knox County Public Schools. He has worked in campaigns, in policy, and in a bowling alley, and brings an ethos of efficacy and the capacity for change to his professional and academic work alike.

Jake comes to Harvard after years on Capitol Hill, so it should come as no surprise that much of his work revolves around Congress. He also studies identity politics and representation more broadly, investigating the intersection of institutions and identity.
