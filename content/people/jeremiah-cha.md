## Jeremiah Cha

**PhD Candidate, Department of Government, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Jeremiah Cha is a PhD Candidate in the Department of Government. He is interested in questions of democratic representation, minority candidate emergence and the U.S. Congress. His current research agenda revolves around the development of the direct primary election and committee politics.
