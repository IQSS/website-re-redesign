<p>This two-hour workshop introduces satellite imagery analysis with Google Earth Engine (GEE) using KNIME’s GEE extension. Specifically, this workshop will: 1) Explain how remote sensing imagery supports Social Science, Health, and Humanities research, 2) Show how to clip and extract imagery and derived products for a user-defined study area, and 3) Walk through a simple land use/land cover classification and basic accuracy check. Students will use the KNIME Analytics Platform with the GEE extension and sample workflows to perform hands-on tasks.</p>
