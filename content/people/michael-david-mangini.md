## Michael-David Mangini

**Government and Economics, GSAS/HKS**

His interests include international and comparative political ecoNomy, especially relating to international institutions and trade. Before coming to Harvard he worked at Bates White EcoNomic Consulting in Washington DC and earned a BA from the University of Pennsylvania.
