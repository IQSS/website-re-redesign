## João Pedro Martins

**PhD student in the Department of Government, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

João Pedro Martins is a PhD student in Government at Harvard University, studying International Relations. His research examines the design and resilience of international organizations within the broader field of international political economy. Methodologically, he is interested in causal inference, survival analysis, and text-as-data.
