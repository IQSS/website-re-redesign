## Sinn Won Han

**Sociology, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Sinn Won is a doctoral student in sociology at Harvard University. Broadly speaking, he is interested in the causes, patterns and consequences of changes in human assortative mating. Specifically, his ongoing research explores the global increase in hypogamy (i.e., women marrying \down\") among highly educated women and investigates what macro-level mechanisms are causing this marriage trend."
