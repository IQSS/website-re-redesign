## Hayley Manges

**PhD Candidate, Department of Government, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Hayley is a PhD candidate in Government at Harvard University. She is broadly interested in the interaction between American political institutions and the mass public with a particular focus on political parties, democratic representation and accountability. Prior to Harvard, she worked in data science at a survey research firm.
