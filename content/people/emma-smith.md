## Emma Smith

**PhD Candidate in Public Policy, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Emma's research interests lie at the intersection of development and behavioral economics. Her current work focuses on understanding the role of social learning in eroding stigma and increasing mental health care-seeking among Syrian refugees in Jordan. She is also a co-founder of the Syrian Refugee Life Study, one of the first long-term longitudinal data collection efforts to survey a large, representative refugee sample and follow that sample over time.
