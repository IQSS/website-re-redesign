<p>IQSS affiliates can book time slots at our office to work and meet.</p>

<p>Whether you are looking to connect with a fellow researcher or colleague, or just need a place to work for the day, we welcome affiliates of IQSS to come join us in our office space.</p>

<p>If you are an IQSS affiliate, you can make a temporary desk reservation using the Robin booking platform. We recommend that you download the Robin Quick Start Guide for a complete overview on how to get started. Particularly important details to note are below.</p>

<p class="button-row"><a class="button" href="../documents/robin_quick_start_guide_iqss_all_affiliates.pdf">Robin Quick Start Guide (PDF)</a></p>

<img class="align-left" src="../images/live/harvard-iqss/files/robin_map_screenshot_1_crop.jpg" alt="Screenshot of room reservation map" />

<h2>Logging in to Robin</h2>

<p>You should log in using your <strong>g.harvard.edu email address </strong>(non-Harvard affiliates should email <a href="mailto:robinhelp@iq.harvard.edu">robinhelp@iq.harvard.edu</a> to be manually added).</p>

<ul><li>Navigate to <a href="https://dashboard.robinpowered.com/iqss/login">the IQSS Robin login page</a>.</li><li>Click “Continue with Google” and log in using your g.harvard.edu credentials.</li></ul>

<h2>Making a Reservation</h2>

<p>Once you have set up your account, you can follow these <a href="https://support.robinpowered.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000403406-Get-to-know-Robin-Desks">instructions for booking a desk</a>.</p>

<ul><li>Please only reserve desks for the time you will be using it.</li><li>Users can book up to 90 days in advance.</li></ul>

<h2 class="clear-floats">Room Access &amp; Security</h2>

<p>When reserving a desk, please send an email to <a href="mailto:robinhelp@iq.harvard.edu">robinhelp@iq.harvard.edu</a> <strong>at least one business day in advance</strong> so that a key can be prepared for you. At the end of the final day of booking, please return keys to the mailbox of the IQSS Office Manager, <a href="{{< relref "people/emma-loayza" >}}">Emma Loayza</a>.</p>

<h2>Questions?</h2>

<p>If you have a question or concern that is not addressed in the <a href="../documents/robin_quick_start_guide_iqss_all_affiliates.pdf">Robin Quick Start Guide for IQSS</a>, please email us at <a href="mailto:robinhelp@iq.harvard.edu">robinhelp@iq.harvard.edu</a>.</p>

<h2>Reserve a desk via mobile app</h2>

<p>In addition to booking via web browser, you can also download the Robin mobile app.</p>
