## Anthony Bald

**PhD Candidate in Public Policy, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Anthony is a PhD candidate in Public Policy. His research interests are in the fields of public economics and labor economics. His work in public economics focuses on the economics of child welfare and impact of foster care on children. In labor economics, he studies the individual and market effects of education policy changes. Prior to joining Harvard, Anthony worked as a predoctoral fellow at Brown University. He holds a B.A. in economics from Pomona College.
