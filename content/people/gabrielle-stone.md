## Gabrielle Stone

**Events Coordinator**

{{< profile-links >}}

She graduated from the University of Connecticut with a Bachelor's degree in English and has a Certificate of Special Studies in Administration and Management from Harvard Extension School, focusing on nonprofit management, marketing and public relations, museum administration, and fundraising. Her previous career was in publishing.
