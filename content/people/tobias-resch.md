## Tobias Resch

**Graduate Student, Department of Government**

{{< profile-links >}}

Tobias Resch is a Ph.D. student in the Department of Government. His research interests include political representation, inequality, and political psychology. He holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Science and Economics from Swarthmore College, where he graduated with high hoNors in 2011. Prior to his graduate studies, he spent two years working as a consultant at McKinsey & Company.
