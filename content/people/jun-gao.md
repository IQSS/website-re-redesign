## Jun Gao

**PhD Candidate in Public Policy, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Jun is a PhD student in Public Policy. Her research interests are in the fields of environmental economics, urban and spatial economics. She studies socioeconomic consequences of policy related to energy markets and transportation. Jun holds an M.Phil. in economics from the University of Oxford and a Bachelor degree from Tsinghua University.
