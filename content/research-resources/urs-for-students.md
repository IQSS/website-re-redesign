<p>Harvard undergraduates can participate in the research mission of the University by working as paid research assistants to IQSS Faculty Affiliates. Faculty funded by the URS program come from a wide array of schools and disciplines at Harvard. There are two ways you can join our program:</p>

<p>1. Check out our <a href="{{< relref "research-resources/urs-research-opportunities" >}}">current job postings</a> for projects in need of research assistants.</p>

<p><strong>-OR-</strong></p>

<p>2. Reach out to a faculty member you would like to work for as a research assistant, and talk to them about <a href="{{< relref "research-resources/urs-how-to-apply" >}}">applying</a> for a URS grant to hire you.</p>
