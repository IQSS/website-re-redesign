## Hannah Castner

**PhD Student in Sociology, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Hannah Castner is a PhD student in Sociology and a Presidential Scholar in the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences. Hannah's research interests surround culture and inequality in educational organizations. Presently, she is interested in the claims parents, teachers, politicians, and administrators make regarding what should be taught in K-12 schools and the consequences of these assertions.
