## Emily Feng

**Undergraduate in Social Studies and Philosophy, Harvard College**

{{< profile-links >}}

Emily Feng is a sophomore at Harvard College pursuing a joint concentration in Social Studies and Philosophy. She is interested in comparative studies of democratization, modes of judicial review, and algorithmic fairness. Emily is currently working with Professor Gary King and Professor Cynthia Dwork on a project related to the Census Bureau's utilization of disclosure avoidance mechanisms.
