## Clem Aeppli

**PhD Candidate in Sociology, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Clem Aeppli studies transformations of the modern workplace—outsourcing and contracting, decentralization, de-unionization—and how these structure income and wealth inequality. In particular, Aeppli is interested in their effect on workers of color.
