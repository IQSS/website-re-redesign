## In Jeong Hwang

**PhD Candidate, Department of Sociology**

{{< profile-links >}}

In Jeong is a PhD student in the Department of Sociology at Harvard University. Her research interests include health, aging, intergenerational relationship, and the work-family interface. She received a BA from UC Berkeley and a MA from Yale University, both in Economics. Prior to graduate studies, she has worked in economic consulting.
