<p>Join us for the virtual launch of the Human Flourishing Program at Harvard’s Flourishing Schools Project. This session will introduce a new effort to support school networks in measuring, reflecting on, and strengthening students’ character skills and overall flourishing.</p>

<p>The event will also provide an overview of the project’s framework and resources, including both a concise project overview and a more detailed implementation guide.</p>

<p>Colleagues across education and research are encouraged to attend and share with others who may find this work relevant.</p>
