## Marc Sabatier Hvidkjær

**IQSS Graduate Student Affiliate**

{{< profile-links >}}

Marc Sabatier Hvidkjær is a PhD student at the Department of Government, studying Comparative Politics and Political Economy. His research interests include how preferences for redistribution are formed and how the welfare state evolves in response to these demands. Prior to Harvard, Marc earned his BSc and MSc in Political Science at the University of Copenhagen.
