## Elysia Larson

**Global Health and Population, HSPH**

{{< profile-links >}}

Elysia Larson is a doctoral student in the Department of Global Health and Population at the Harvard T. H. Chan School of Public Health. Elysia's research interests focus on improving health system quality and responsiveness in low and middle income countries. Her research explores the ways in which different stakeholders in the health system, particularly communities, define and influence health system quality. Elysia uses epidemiologic and ecoNometric methods to evaluate health systems interventions and reforms.Elysia received her MPH in epidemiology and international health from Boston University and her BSc in biology and mathematics from McGill University. She currently manages a NIH-funded, cluster-randomized maternal and newborn health study in Pwani Region, Tanzania (PI: Margaret Kruk) and has been involved in field work in Tanzania, Kenya, Zambia, and South Africa.
