<p>The annual <a href="https://gis.harvard.edu/">Center for Geographic Analysis</a> conference will be held this year on <strong>October 2nd and 3rd</strong>, 2026. This conference is one of CGA’s signature events, attracting spatial researchers and practitioners from across campus and around the globe. Our 2026 conference will be no exception, except for one key detail: it will also be our 20th birthday!</p>

<p>In recognition of the occasion, the theme of this year’s conference is “<em>Celebrating Geographic Analysis: Past, Present, and Future</em>”. We’ll be seeking contributions from across geography, the social sciences, and the humanities that illuminate, spark discussion, and point the way to the methods, questions, and data required for impactful and exciting research that increases our understanding of the world around us and helps improve it.</p>

<p>We are pulling out all the stops and invite you to be part of the excitement. <strong>The conference will be held October 2nd and 3rd</strong>, and will feature two days of keynotes, talks, panels, and workshops. And of course, as always, there will be lots of time for informal networking.</p>

<h2><strong>Conference Schedule Overview</strong></h2>

<p>Workshops (free to conference registrants with a separate registration) will be held the morning of October 2, 9am–12pm. The main conference will open at 1pm in the Rubenstein Treehouse on October 2, with plenary keynote and sessions until 6pm. On October 3, the conference will run approximately 9am–6pm, with lunch and coffee breaks provided for all conference registrants.</p>

<p>View the <a href="https://harvard-cga.github.io/cga-conference-2026/">preliminary conference program</a>. This will be updated on about a weekly basis leading up to the conference. View the <a href="https://gis.harvard.edu/2026-cga-conference-workshops">conference workshop descriptions and details</a>. Workshop registration is open to any conference participant who has registered and paid.</p>

<h2><strong>Venue</strong></h2>

<p>Harvard's <a href="https://rubensteintreehouse.harvard.edu/">David Rubenstein Treehouse</a>, located across the river from CGA’s offices, in the Enterprise Research Campus, directly across Western Ave. from the Harvard Business School.</p>

<h2><strong>Registration (now closed)</strong></h2>

<p>Registration fees are $25 for Harvard students, $50 for Harvard non-students, $100 for non-Harvard students, and $175 for non-Harvard, non-students (regular attendees). Registration is now closed, we are at capacity.</p>

<h2><strong>Abstract Submission&nbsp;(now closed)</strong></h2>

<p>We welcome presentation submissions on any contemporary geographic analysis topic, including applied research, data, methods, and training. Given limited space on the program, accepted abstracts may be accepted for full presentation, lightning talk, or poster.<br>&nbsp;</p>

<h2><strong>Panel and Workshop Proposal Submission (now closed)</strong></h2>

<p>This year we are excited to welcome proposals for workshops and panels. We are especially keen to receive expressions of interest in the areas of curriculum development, applied spatial machine learning and AI, methods and data training, and early-career professional development.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Questions, ideas? Feel free to <a href="https://gis.harvard.edu/contactus">contact CGA</a>. We are happy to engage!</p>
