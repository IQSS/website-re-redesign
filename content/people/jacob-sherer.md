## Jacob Sherer

**PhD Student, Department of Government, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Jacob Sherer is a PhD student in the Department of Government, with a focus on American Politics and Political Methodology. His research interests lie broadly at the intersection of American politics, political geography, and political behavior. Prior to Harvard, Jacob earned a BA in Political Science from the University of Notre Dame, and an MPhil in Politics and International Studies from the University of Cambridge. He is originally from Platteville, Wisconsin.
