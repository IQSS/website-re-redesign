## Anny Tazio Hal Rubinson Fenton

**Sociology, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Anny is a doctoral candidate in Sociology and a doctoral fellow in Inequality and Social Policy at the Harvard Kennedy School. She is a multi-method researcher and her dissertation combines ethNography in pediatric offices, interviews, and pediatric patient visit recordings to investigate why HPV vaccination rates are so low in the US. Her other research interests include discrimination, the sharing ecoNomy, and sexuality.
