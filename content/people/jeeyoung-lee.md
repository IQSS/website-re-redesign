## Jeeyoung Lee

**PhD Student, Government**

{{< profile-links >}}

Jeeyoung Lee is a Ph.D. student in the Department of Government at Harvard University studying comparative political economy with broad interests in inequality studies. Her research focuses on housing politics, private alternatives to welfare, the effect of increasing financialization and privatization on policy preferences, and the distributive outcomes of market policies and welfare policies.
