## Siri Neerchal

**PhD Student in Sociology and Social Policy, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Siri Neerchal is a PhD student in Sociology and Social Policy at Harvard. Their research interests include intersecting inequalities in work, labor, occupations, and health (with a particular focus on LGBTQ and gender-diverse populations) and broader operationalizations of gender in quantitative research. Siri is a National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellow and a James M. and Cathleen D. Stone PhD Scholar in Inequality and Wealth Concentration.
