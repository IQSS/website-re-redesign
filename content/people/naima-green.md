## Naima Green

**Government, GSAS**

I am a PhD student in the Department of Government focusing on international relations. I am interested in political psychology and the impact of public opinion and preferences upon foreign policy. In particular, I study U.S.-China relations, the role of powerful states in the international system, and the interactions between mass publics and interstate relations.
