## Georgina Evans

**PhD Candidate, Department of Government**

{{< profile-links >}}

Georgina is PhD student in the Department of Government and  AM candidate in the Department of Statistics at Harvard. Her research interests are in comparative politics and political methodology, with a focus on electoral politics in Europe and other advanced democracies. Prior to coming to Harvard, Georgina earned a BA in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics from the University of Oxford.
