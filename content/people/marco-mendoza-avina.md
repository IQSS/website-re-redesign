## Marco Mendoza Aviña

**PhD Student, Department of Government**

{{< profile-links >}}

Marco Mendoza Aviña is a PhD student in Government and Social Policy. His research interests include public opinion, racial and ethnic politics, immigration, inequality, urban politics, survey methodology, and experiments. Prior to Harvard, Marco completed bachelor's and master's degrees in political science at l'Université de Montréal.
