## Mohammad Al-Mailam

**Graduate Student, Master of Arts Regional Studies--Middle East**

{{< profile-links >}}

Mohammad is a second-year graduate student interested in political economy, migration, and ethnic politics. His research draws on archival methods and survey experiments to study institutional change and identitarian political behavior in the Middle East. Before Harvard, Mohammad was a James C. Gaither Junior Fellow at the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace.
