## Isabel Prioleau

**PhD Student, Department of Government, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Isabel Prioleau is a PhD student and Carl J. Friedrich Fellow in the Department of Government at Harvard University, where she studies the comparative politics of democracy in Europe and North America. Her research explores the political economy and geography of democratic backsliding, both historical and contemporary. Isabel received a B.A. in Political Science from Yale University in 2025.
