## Yenee Soh

**Research Associate, Social and Behavioral Sciences, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health**

{{< profile-links >}}

 

Yenee Soh, ScD, SM, is a social epidemiologist and research associate in the Department of Social and Behavioral Sciences at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. Dr. Soh earned her doctorate in Social and Behavioral Sciences and master’s in Epidemiology from the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. She also holds a bachelor’s degree in Mathematics from Macalester College. Previously, she was a post-doctoral research fellow at the Kaiser Permanente Northern California Division of Research. 

Dr. Soh’s research interests are broadly focused on the social determinants of population health and health disparities across the life course, applying causal inference methodology. Specifically, her work has examined the role of social and psychosocial factors on cardiovascular morbidities, dementia and cognitive decline among middle-aged and older adults. She also has extensive experience working with prospective observational cohorts across multiple countries. Her current research also examines the intergenerational transmission of disease risk within families, factors related to early childhood development, and dyadic methodology to better capture couple and family dynamics – with a particular focus on neurodevelopment and psychopathology.
