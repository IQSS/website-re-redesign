## Yuning Zhuang

**Visiting Fellow, China Biographical Database**

{{< profile-links >}}

Yuning Zhuang is a PhD candidate in art theory and currently a visiting fellow of the China Biographical Database Project (CBDB). Her research focuses on the social networks and spatial distributions of ancient Chinese artists.
