## António Câmara

**PhD Candidate in Government, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

António Câmara is a PhD student in the Department of Government. His research is at the intersection of politics and computing. Methodologically, he is interested in using and creating machine learning, natural language processing, and game theoretic models to study political behavior and public opinion. Substantively, he is interested in the relationship between technology and politics, including the influence, perception, and regulation of traditional media organizations, social media services, and other emerging technologies. Prior to Harvard, he earned a BS in Computer Science with a minor in Political Science at Columbia University.
