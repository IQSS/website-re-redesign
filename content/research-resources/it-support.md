### Get help from HUIT

As of August 10, 2026, IT support services previously managed by the IQSS Client Support Services (CSS) team has transitioned to Harvard University Information Technology (HUIT) centralized support.

Members of the CGIS community can now request IT support through the HUIT Service Desk through any of the following means:

- Call 617-495-7777
- Submit a Ticket using the [HUIT Service Desk form](http://harvard.service-now.com/ithelp?id=submit_ticket&sys_id=3f1dd0320a0a0b99000a53f7604a2ef9)
- Email: [ithelp@harvard.edu](mailto:ithelp@harvard.edu)
- Chat: Immediate support via chat at the [IT Help main page](https://huit.harvard.edu/ithelp)
- In-Person: Visit the [Walk-In Support Center](https://harvard.service-now.com/ithelp?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=7b1885d01b1a6c101a5376e2cd4bcb1a) at the Science Center
