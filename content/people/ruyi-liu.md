## Ruyi Liu

**Graduate Student in Regional Studies - East Asia, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Ruyi Liu is a graduate student in the Regional Studies - East Asia program. Her academic interests include the study of international organizations and how states navigate global governance during power transitions, with an empirical focus on East Asia. She earned a BSc in International Relations with First-Class Honors from the London School of Economics and Political Science.
