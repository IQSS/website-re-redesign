## Yizhou Jin

**GSAS + HBS, Economics**

{{< profile-links >}}

Yizhou holds a BA degree in Mathematics and Economics at UC Berkeley. He also worked for Citigroup, Houlihan Lokey, and SiNochem Petroleum before coming to Harvard.
