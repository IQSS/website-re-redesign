## Siqin (Sisi) Wang

**Associate Chair, Spatial Data Lab, Harvard CGA**

{{< profile-links >}}

Dr Wang's research interest and expertise lie in developing and applying methods and frameworks of geospatial science and spatiotemporal big data analytics (e.g., AI) to solve real-world problems in digital health geography, environmental health, population-health-environment interactions, and computation social science more broadly.
