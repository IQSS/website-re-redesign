## Linh Vu

**IQSS Undergraduate Research Scholar**

Linh Vu is a junior at Harvard College studying Statistics and Government, with a secondary in Educational Studies. She is currently assisting Professor Pia Raffler on a project on the power dynamics between local politicians and bureaucrats in developing nations. She is interested in applying her statistical training to causes in poverty alleviation and education.
