## Andrea Passalacqua

**Economics, GSAS ad HKS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Andrea Passalacqua is a third year PhD student in political ecoNomy and Government - Econ track. His research interests are in Corporate Finance, InNovation and Political EcoNomy. One of his project investigates the returns from lobbying both from the perspective of the lobbying firm and the firm investing in lobbying. Andrea earned a B.A. and a M.Sc in Economics from Bocconi University.
