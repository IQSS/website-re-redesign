## Julia Smith Coyoli

**Graduate Student in Government, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

My research interests are in Comparative Politics and Political Economy, with a focus on the political economy of primary and secondary education and Latin American politics. Prior to beginning my studies at Harvard I obtained a BA in Latin American Studies and Educational Studies at Macalester College (St. Paul, MN) and an MSc in Economics from the Instituto Tecnológico Autónomo de México (Mexico City, Mexico).
