## Stephanie Kestelman

**PhD Student in Economics, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Stephanie is a PhD student in economics. She employs methods in applied econometrics, text analysis and industrial organization to address questions in urban public finance and economic geography. Among her interests are the design of place-based policies, the spillovers of housing and infrastructure policies, and geographic path dependence in urban contexts.
