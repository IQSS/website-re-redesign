## Michael Bar-Sinai

**Associate of IQSS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Michael Bar-Sinai holds a BSc in software engineer from the Technion, and is working on his PhD dissertation in Ben Gurion University of the Negev, Israel. 

He is interested in the impact software systems have on society, such as privacy issues and filter bubbles. A bit of a programming language geek (that's his dissertation focal point) Michael is also interested in Big Data and massively parallel computations. Also, he is into general hackery, wherever it may be applied.
