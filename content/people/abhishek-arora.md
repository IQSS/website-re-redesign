## Abhishek Arora

**PhD student, Department of Economics, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Abhishek is a PhD student in Economics at Harvard interested in Political Economy and Development. He uses and develops deep learning-powered tools and techniques to answer questions in these areas. Before joining the program, he worked as a Predoctoral Fellow at Harvard University and as a consultant at the World Bank and the Indian Statistical Institute, Delhi. He holds an MA in Economics from the Delhi School of Economics.
