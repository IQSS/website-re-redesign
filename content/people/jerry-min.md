## Jerry Min

**PhD Candidate in Government, GSAS**

{{< profile-links >}}

Jerry Min is a PhD candidate at the Government Department as well as an AM candidate at the Statistics Department. His fields of study are International/Comparative Political Economy and Quantitative Methodology. He is particularly interested in the politics of international investment, international law and organizations, identity politics, and East Asia (especially China and Japan). Prior to coming to Harvard, Jerry was a Research Data Analyst at Stanford University’s Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies. He holds an MA from Stanford University and a BA from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
