## Motohiko Adomi

**PhD Candidate, Department of Epidemiology, HSPH**

{{< profile-links >}}

Motohiko Adomi is a PhD student in Epidemiology at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. His research interests are in causal inference methods applied to clinical decision-making, including the estimation of average and heterogeneous treatment effects for medications, as well as modified treatment policies using observational data.
