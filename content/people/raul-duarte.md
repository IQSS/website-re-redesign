## Raúl Duarte

**PhD Candidate in Political Economy and Government**

{{< profile-links >}}

Raúl Duarte is a Ph.D. student in Political Economy and Government. His research interests span political economy, economic development, comparative politics, and public finance. Among his specific interests are public service delivery, state capacity, social networks, and clientelism. Before his doctoral studies, he graduated from Harvard Kennedy School's MPA/ID program and from Washington University in St. Louis where he studied Economics and Mathematics.
